# How to Create a Strong Password

## Introduction
A strong password protects your account from hackers and unauthorized access.

## Steps

1. Use at least 12 characters in your password.
2. Include uppercase and lowercase letters.
3. Add numbers and special symbols (like @, #, $).
4. Avoid using common words or personal information (like your name or birthday).
5. Use a different password for each account.
6. Consider using a password manager to store passwords safely.

## Tips
- Avoid using "123456" or "password" — these are the most common weak passwords.
- Change your password every few months for better security.
- Never share your password with anyone, even friends.
