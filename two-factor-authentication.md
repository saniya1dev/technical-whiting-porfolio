# How to Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

## Introduction
Two-Factor Authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account.

## Steps

1. Go to your account Settings.
2. Click on "Security" or "Privacy".
3. Find the option "Two-Factor Authentication" and click "Enable".
4. Choose a method — SMS, Email, or Authenticator App.
5. Enter your phone number or scan the QR code (for authenticator apps).
6. Enter the verification code sent to confirm setup.
7. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

## Tips
- Authenticator apps are more secure than SMS.
- Never share your verification code with anyone.
- Save backup codes in case you lose access to your phone.
