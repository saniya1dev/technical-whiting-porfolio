# How to Reset a Forgotten Password

## Introduction
This guide explains how to reset your password if you forget it.

## Steps

1. Go to the login page of the app or website.
2. Click "Forgot Password" below the login button.
3. Enter the email address linked to your account.
4. Check your email inbox for a password reset link.
5. Click the link and enter a new password.
6. Confirm the new password and click "Save".

## Tips
- Choose a strong password with letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Do not share your password reset link with anyone.
- Check your spam folder if you don't see the email.
