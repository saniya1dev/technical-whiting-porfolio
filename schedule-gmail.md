# How to Schedule an Email in Gmail

## Introduction
Gmail lets you write an email now and send it automatically at a later time.

## Steps

1. Open Gmail and click "Compose" to write a new email.
2. Write your email — recipient, subject, and message.
3. Click the small arrow next to the "Send" button.
4. Select "Schedule send" from the menu.
5. Choose a date and time for the email to be sent.
6. Click "Schedule Send" to confirm.

## Tips
- Scheduled emails can be found in the "Scheduled" folder.
- You can cancel or edit a scheduled email before it is sent.
- Useful for sending emails during business hours across time zones.
