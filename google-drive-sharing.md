# How to Share Files Using Google Drive

## Introduction
Google Drive allows you to share files and folders with others easily.

## Steps

1. Open Google Drive and log in to your account.
2. Right-click on the file or folder you want to share.
3. Click "Share" from the menu.
4. Enter the email address of the person you want to share with.
5. Choose their permission level — Viewer, Commenter, or Editor.
6. Click "Send" to share the file.

## Alternative: Share via Link
1. Right-click the file and click "Share".
2. Click "Copy link".
3. Change the setting to "Anyone with the link".
4. Send the link to the person.

## Tips
- Use "Viewer" access if you don't want others to edit the file.
- Always double-check the email address before sharing sensitive files.
