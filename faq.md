# FAQs: Music App

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this app free to use?**
A: Yes, the basic version is free. Premium features require a subscription.

**Q: How do I create a playlist?**
A: Tap the "+" icon on the home screen and select "New Playlist".

**Q: Can I download songs for offline listening?**
A: Yes, premium users can download songs by tapping the download icon.

**Q: How do I cancel my subscription?**
A: Go to Settings, tap "Subscription", and select "Cancel Plan".
